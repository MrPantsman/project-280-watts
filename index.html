About reconciliation and limitations

Past planned workouts are not counted as actual work: a shorter recorded ride replaces the plan, and an unrecorded ride contributes zero recorded work (subject to sync checks). Unpaired rides are included. Completed strength activities are conservatively estimated using your configured strength allowance, not Garmin/MFP calorie figures. Previous targets are calculated from the exported calendar as it stands now; this is not a permanent historical snapshot. Apple Health totals have no meal labels, so food completeness and on-bike intake cannot be inferred. On-bike carbohydrate amounts come from Intervals.icu CHO In and are displayed separately; Apple Health food totals are not adjusted because the export does not identify whether ride fuel was logged in MyFitnessPal. Weight attached to an activity may be carried forward rather than newly measured. GitHub exports are loaded into this browser; they are also stored in your GitHub repository.

How the guidance works

Carbohydrate guides vary with duration and intensity and include on-bike fuel. Protein guide: approximately 1.6–2.0 g/kg/day. These are starting ranges, not a diagnosis of low energy availability. A missing or incomplete food diary is never treated as zero intake. Exercise energy estimates are not added to food intake. Completed cycling work and MFP food are compared in the retrospective review. The baseline energy model remains provisional.
