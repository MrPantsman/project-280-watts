How the guidance works

Carbohydrate guides vary with duration and intensity and include on-bike fuel. Protein guide: approximately 1.6–2.0 g/kg/day. These are starting ranges, not a diagnosis of low energy availability. A missing or incomplete food diary is never treated as zero intake. Exercise energy estimates are not added to food intake. The daily energy planner estimates calories from imported planned workouts; it does not automatically sync or yet reconcile completed rides and MyFitnessPal food.
